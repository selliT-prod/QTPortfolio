Act I — About

About Me

I'm a California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo student pursuing a career in live event production, with a particular interest in concert production and stage management. Through hands-on experience with live events, I've supported talent coordination, backstage logistics, security, credentials, and communication between artists and production teams.

I'm drawn to live production because it demands teamwork, adaptability, and the ability to solve problems in real time. I enjoy working with people, taking responsibility, and learning how every part of a production comes together. I'm eager to build technical knowledge across staging, lighting, audio, and event operations while developing the experience necessary to eventually lead productions and teams.

My background in digital marketing, audio production, and event operations has also given me a broader perspective on how entertainment projects are created, promoted, and delivered. I'm early in my career, but I'm ambitious, highly motivated, and ready to learn from experienced professionals in the live entertainment industry.
